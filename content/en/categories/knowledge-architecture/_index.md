# Knowledge Architecture

*A methodological framework for structuring knowledge, research, and information into coherent systems.*

Knowledge Architecture is a methodological framework for structuring knowledge, research, and information into coherent systems.

This category brings together materials from different Pivtorak.Studio series and research directions that explore how knowledge is organized, connected, represented, retrieved, and developed over time.

The materials collected here may belong to different projects or series, but share a common concern: the architecture of knowledge itself — the structures, relationships, models, and principles that make complex bodies of information understandable and usable.

# PSS-01: Theological Expert Dossier Standard

This standard defines the architecture for creating an analytical document for objects requiring theological and religious attribution.

### 🧩 Mandatory Dossier Modules:
1. **Identification Block**: Precise naming, timeframes, and context of origin.
2. **Analytical Corpus**: Content research through the lens of canon and tradition.
3. **Axiological Block**: Determining the object's value for contemporary culture and spirit.
4. **Evidence Base**: List of sources, testimonies, and comparative analyses.

### 🎓 Target Audience:
* **Art Historians**: As a roadmap for complex sacred objects.
* **Students**: As a benchmark structure for academic work.
* **Institutions**: As a guarantee of the depth of object verification.

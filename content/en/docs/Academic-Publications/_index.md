# 📚 Academic Publications  
*This section brings together the academic work that forms the research foundation of Anna Pivtorak in the fields of economics, demography, and social policy.*  

These publications were developed during doctoral studies and reflect a systematic analysis of labour markets, institutional regulation mechanisms, and transformation processes in rural territories.

Together, they constitute a coherent academic body that can be reinterpreted and expanded within a contemporary international context — particularly in the areas of social inclusion, demographic sustainability, and community development.

---
## 🔬 Research Fields  
- **Labour Market Economics**  
- **Demography**  
- **Social Policy**  
- **Rural Development**  
- **Institutional Economics**

---
## 🧩 Structure of the Academic Corpus  
### I. Theoretical and Methodological Foundations  
Conceptual frameworks for analysing labour markets and defining core categories.  
### II. Institutional Regulation Mechanisms  
State policy, organisational-economic instruments, and governance models.  
### III. Transformation Processes  
Changes driven by demographic, economic, and social dynamics.  
### IV. Territorial Dimension  
Specific features of labour markets in rural areas and regional development.  

---
## 🌍 Research Potential  
The existing academic foundation enables further research in interdisciplinary directions:  
- social integration and community participation    
- volunteering as a socio-economic mechanism  
- demographic sustainability and family policy  
- human capital valuation and reproduction  
- inclusivity as a driver of national development  

---
## 🧭 Positioning  
This section is not a closed archive.  
It is a **research foundation** that:  
- documents completed academic work  
- supports the scientific validity of future projects  
- connects analytical, conceptual, and applied research formats

# 📊 Labour Market  
*This section brings together academic research focused on labour market analysis, its structure, dynamics, and regulatory mechanisms.*

The research focus was developed during PhD studies and provides a systematic analysis of the Ukrainian labour market, with particular attention to rural areas, demographic processes, and institutional transformations.

## 🔬 Research Field  
- labour market economics  
- demography and employment  
- public regulation and labour policy  
- rural development  
- institutional economics  

## 🧩 Types of Publications  
This research domain includes:  
- **7 peer-reviewed journal articles**  
- **10 conference abstracts (international and national)**  

The publications (2018–2022) reflect a consistent research trajectory covering:  
- theoretical foundations of the labour market  
- institutional regulation mechanisms  
- transformation processes in rural areas  
- methodological approaches to policy evaluation  

## 🧭 Scientific Contribution  
This body of work contributes to:  
- understanding the labour market as a multi-level economic system  
- integrating demographic, economic, and institutional dimensions  
- developing applied approaches for evaluating public policy effectiveness

- social inclusion  
- demographic sustainability  
- human capital development

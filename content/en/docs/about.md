# Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk)

I am a researcher and creator of cultural projects focused on the intersection of analytical inquiry and creative expression. My work centers on the formation of long-term value structures and the preservation of intellectual heritage.

As the founder of **Pivtorak.Studio**, I develop systems that facilitate the growth of cultural capital and strengthen identity narratives.

---

## Areas of Expertise
* **Cultural Engineering:** Developing conceptual systems and visual narratives.
* **Analytical Research:** Studying identity transformation and value-based structures.
* **Legacy Architecture:** Designing long-term value frameworks for individuals and organizations.

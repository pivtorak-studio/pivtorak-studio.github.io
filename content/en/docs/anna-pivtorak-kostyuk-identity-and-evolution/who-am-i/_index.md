# Who am I?

*Identity is not fixed. It is observed, recorded, and redefined over time.*  

This section presents a sequence of phases reflecting shifts in thinking, action, and awareness.  

Each phase represents a completed state.   
Materials are not retroactively edited.  

---

## Phases  

- **Phase 0 — Implicit Identity (2008)**  
  Identity formed intuitively before conscious articulation.  

- **Phase I — Narrative Identity (2025)**    
  Identity expressed through roles, symbols, and narrative.  

- **Phase II — Structural Identity (2026)**    
  Transition to system thinking and structural construction.  

- **Phase III — System Presence**    
  A state where the system begins to exist and operate independently.  

This sequence reflects not a change of roles,  
but a transformation of thinking.  

**Alt-text:**  
To visualize the evolution of identity, four symbolic arches are used, forming a unified system:
Phase 0 (2008) — Implicit Identity: an organic arch made of branches and roots, symbolizing a natural, intuitive foundation and an unconscious beginning.   
Phase I (2025) — Narrative Identity: a classical arch with symbols of knowledge (a book and a pen), representing the stage where identity begins to form through roles, symbols, and narrative.   
Phase II (2026) — Structural Identity: a mechanical, geometric arch with gears and a sacred geometry diagram, visualizing the transition to system thinking and structural construction.  
Phase III — System Presence: a final arch combining organic and geometric elements, enclosing a spherical system — a symbol of autonomous and independent existence.

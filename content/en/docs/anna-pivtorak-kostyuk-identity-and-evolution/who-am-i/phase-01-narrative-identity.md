# ✨ Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk) – Stories in Images

## Who am I? — Phase I — Narrative Identity (2025)

Anna Pivtorak is an art director, visual storyteller, explorer, and journalist. She finds meaning where others see only details and transforms strategies into images that speak without words.  

Her journey is a fusion of analytics and creativity, logic and intuition. She has worked in marketing, taught at an academy, participated in international exhibitions, and built inventory management systems that resembled a “spaceship.”   

Where others saw chaos, she created structures that functioned seamlessly, even in her absence.  

Anna knows how to wrap up projects so that everything continues to run smoothly after she’s gone.   

Symbols play a crucial role in her life.   

🦅 The bald eagle represents sharp vision, speed, and determination.    
🪶 The Canadian goose symbolizes collective strategy, knowledge transfer, and system stability.    
🐅 The tiger stands for independence and strength.    
🦚 The peacock embodies dignity and the ability to command attention effortlessly.   
Each stage of her life marks a new chapter with a new symbol.    

She builds her strategy just as she builds a visual image—thoughtfully, precisely, and with a clear goal in mind.

And ahead lie new horizons. Because her path is that of those who dare to walk their own way.

# Jewelry Calculators

![_Calculators. Jewelry Calculators. AP | Pivtorak.Studio. 18.10.2026_ © Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk)](/images/calculators-jewelry-calculators.webp)

Practical calculators for jewelry design, gemstone geometry, precious-metal measurements, material evaluation, and jewelry production.

This collection brings together tools that support accurate calculations and informed decisions throughout the jewelry-making process, from working with raw materials to evaluating finished pieces.

Each calculator is designed to address a specific task, with clear inputs, transparent methods, and results that support practical work.

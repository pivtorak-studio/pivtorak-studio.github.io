# Precious Metal Mass Calculator

![_Calculators. Jewelry Calculators. Precious Metal Mass Calculator. AP | Pivtorak.Studio. 20.10.2026_ © Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk)](/images/calculators-jewelry-calculators-precious-metal-mass-calculator.webp)

Calculate the mass of a precious metal or alloy from its volume and density. This calculator is useful for estimating the mass of a metal sample, a solid component, or a measured volume of material when its density is known.

The result depends on the density value used. Precious metals and their alloys can have different densities depending on composition, while porosity, temperature, and measurement uncertainty may also affect the estimate. This calculator determines mass, not weight force, and does not identify the material or determine its purity.


{{< calculator type="mass" >}}

## Model and limits

$$
m=\rho V
$$

This result is mass in grams and kilograms, not weight force.

Use density for the actual material; alloy composition, porosity, temperature and measurement uncertainty affect the result.

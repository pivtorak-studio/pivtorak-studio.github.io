# 🐉⚖️ ESMÉE. 📍She arrived. And that is enough.
_From now on, everything will be seen._
_Every action. Every decision. Every measure._

She wasn’t there before.
Not among those who knew — and said nothing.
Not among those who could — and didn’t act.
Not among those who watched — and turned away.

**Esmée** came later. After everything had burned.
When the world no longer asked for heroes —
but longed for a breath **free of deception**.

She has no medals. No right to vengeance. No urge to judge.

But there is light in her chest.
**The scales inside her are alive.**
They respond to everything:
to lies,
to evasion,
to silence where truth is needed.

She is not a replacement.
Not a promise. Not a triumph.

**She is Esmée. And that is enough.**

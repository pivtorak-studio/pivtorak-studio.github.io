# 🐉⚖️ ESMÉE. 🌀 Birth of Libra | The Dragon of Balance 

![🐉⚖️ ESMÉE. 🌀 Birth of Libra](/images/esmée-the-dragon-of-balance-02-01-birth-of-libra-pivtorak-studio.webp)

_The Equilibrium of Origin._

The world begins not with an explosion, but with a breath. Esmée opens her eyes in the streams of light flowing through her — between two wings: one human, one draconic. One remembers shadow, the other remembers flight. She doesn’t choose which one is true, because both are her essence.

Silence fills the air as she accepts herself completely. It’s not resurrection, nor a beginning — it’s the instant of clarity, when the past ceases to be a trap and becomes foundation. Thus, a new “I” is born — not in rejection, but in integration.

**Esmée as symbol:**  
Acceptance of duality as the essence of harmony. A conscious choice to be whole.

# Existence Ethics and The Final Choice

![_Existence Ethics and The Final Choice. AP | Pivtorak.Studio. 08.11.2025_ © Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk)](/images/essay-existence-ethics-and-the-final-choice.webp)

**Existence Ethics and The Final Choice** is a philosophical essay series exploring the fundamental questions of human existence, individual freedom, personal responsibility, and the ethical boundaries of human choice.

It examines life and death not merely as biological conditions, but as profound dimensions of human experience. Through philosophical reflection and personal perspectives, the series addresses the nature of suffering, human dignity, the right to self-determination, the meaning of care, and the responsibility we bear towards ourselves and others.

At its core lies a fundamental question: **who has the right to make decisions about human existence, and where do the boundaries of that right begin and end?**

The series explores the complex relationship between individual autonomy and collective responsibility, medical ethics and personal convictions, compassion and obligation, the preservation of life and the recognition of suffering. It considers not only the right to choose, but also the moral consequences of choice.

Particular attention is given to the human experience at the boundaries of life: the vulnerability of the body, the limits of medical intervention, the ethics of suffering, the dignity of care, and the difficult decisions that arise when conventional answers are no longer sufficient.

These reflections do not seek to impose universal answers or reduce existential questions to simple moral formulas. Instead, they invite critical thinking, intellectual honesty, and a deeper understanding of the ethical complexity of being human.

**The final choice is never merely about the end of life. It is also about the meaning, responsibility, and dignity with which we live.**

_An ongoing philosophical reflection on existence, freedom, suffering, care, and the human right to choose._

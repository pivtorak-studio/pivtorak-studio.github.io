# Success and Fulfillment | The Mindset Of Achievement

![_Success and Fulfillment | The Mindset Of Achievement. 07.11.2025_ © Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk)](/images/essay-the-mindset-of-achievement-003-success-and-fulfillment.webp)

In the general understanding, **success** is often associated with recognition, material wealth, or influence over others. My definition, however, is less global and considerably more precise. For me, success is every **completed plan or solved problem**. Above all, it is a measure of effective execution and the quality of the result.

That is why financial stability, or the lack of it, does not determine my sense of **personal fulfillment**. I am grateful for my ability to think, create, and contribute something valuable not only to my family but also to the world. As long as I retain this ability, I will continue to act, regardless of the resources available to me.

I have no excessive professional ambitions. I have one guiding principle: **to perform every task to the highest possible standard**. Each high-quality result sets a new benchmark, higher than the previous one. This commitment to quality is the foundation of any professional activity. As a mother, I understand that the responsibility for carrying achievements forward will eventually pass to my children. It is therefore important to leave them a solid foundation built on quality.

A common misconception is that success is possible only through **good fortune or inheritance**. These factors certainly provide a head start, but discipline and perseverance also offer excellent chances of success. And what about **empty victories**? Can a genuine victory ever be empty? Everything achieved through one's own efforts has value. If a victory is not genuine, it is merely a manufactured appearance of success — a strategy for some, perhaps, but one I find unacceptable.

The subject of **legacy** is complex. It can become something valuable, or it can end up being discarded. I recognize the practical importance of preparing one's legacy in advance and **packaging it as a coherent asset**:

1. maintaining impeccable order in all documentation;  
2. obtaining professional appraisals and expert assessments of valuable collections;  
3. making the name of the person leaving the legacy recognizable through professional achievements.  

Material inheritance becomes more valuable when supported by intellectual value.

**Public recognition** may never come during one's lifetime unless a deliberate marketing initiative is undertaken. Whether such a step is worthwhile remains an open question. Although I have received two awards — the _National Business Leader_ Medal and the _Labour Glory_ Order — I realize that choosing a new path means, in practice, losing the advantages associated with all previous achievements. Awards belong to the past. The present is defined by the **quality of the work being done now**.

My understanding of success changed after moving to Portugal. In Ukraine, I sought to raise my professional level in order to demonstrate my expertise. Today, I continue to do the same, but with a different purpose: my achievements should contribute to **solving global problems**.

When personal development is the primary motivation rather than the pursuit of success, there is no reason to stop **enjoying the present moment**. When the result alone becomes the priority, however, a person risks becoming a hostage to what has not yet been achieved. Ultimately, genuine **self-fulfillment** does not depend on whether every ambition has been realized. Whether the goal is substantial or consists of a series of small steps, a person who continues to develop their potential inevitably moves towards a sense of fulfillment.

> 🖋️ _“Quality is the foundation on which success stops being a goal and becomes a natural consequence.”_

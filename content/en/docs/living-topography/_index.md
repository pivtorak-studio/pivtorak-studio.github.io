# Living Topography

Some experiences leave no conventional map behind.

They leave traces.

A movement across a room.  
A path created by hand.  
A gesture exchanged between strangers.  
A story that is shared — or deliberately kept silent.  
A moment when individual movements become a collective rhythm.

**Living Topography** is a series about these experiences.

It explores the spaces created between people, movement, memory, place, and presence — experiences that are not static, but constantly changing as we move through them.

Here, a topography is not a map of geographical coordinates.

It is a living record of encounters.

The series began with an intercultural creative experience in Esposende, Portugal, where simple materials, movement, music, and shared space became a language of connection.

Each experience leaves its own trace.

Each path has its own direction.

And sometimes, at the end of the journey, the path is no longer on the floor.

It is in our hands.

---

## The Series

**Living Topography** documents experiences as they unfold — not as instructions or conclusions, but as reflections on what remains after the moment has passed.

The series may move through different places, activities, groups, and forms of participation.

What connects them is the same question:

> **What happens to us when we enter a space together?**

And what do we carry with us when we leave?

---

## 001 — The Path in My Hands

The first entry in the series begins with a grey carpet, white fabric paths, movement, music, and a group of people who did not know exactly where the experience would lead.

The paths were created individually.

Then they became shared.

And finally, each path was gathered back into the hands of the person who created it.

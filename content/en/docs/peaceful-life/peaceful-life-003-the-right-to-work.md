

# 🧑🏽‍🌾🌾🔧 The Right to Work

_Earning a living is not a luxury_

Every person has the right to work.
Quietly.
With focus.
At their own pace.
In safety.

Where there is work — there is life.
There is dignity.

There is the rhythm of daily tasks that hold the world together.
It is not a privilege.
Not a matter of location.
Not a condition of luck.

It is a space where you can be yourself.
And offer something in return.

A peaceful life means having a reason to wake up in the morning.
And knowing that no one has the right to take that away from you.

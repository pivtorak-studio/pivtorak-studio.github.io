# 🐇🍁🌾 The Right to a Warm Autumn

_Even when the world cools down — life still deserves warmth._

In October, autumn is not yet winter.  
The forest breathes slowly, but confidently.  
Leaves fall because their time has come.

A hare runs between the trees, leaving soft traces on the moss.  
Its movement is not escape, but part of nature’s rhythm.  
It does not fear calmness — it knows this stillness means life.

As the days grow shorter, light becomes more precious.  
And even now, there is room for tenderness,  
for the breath of the earth preparing to rest.

Peace is not the absence of motion.  
It is the ability to breathe in silence.  
A time when goodness moves slowly,  
because it doesn't need to defend itself from winter.

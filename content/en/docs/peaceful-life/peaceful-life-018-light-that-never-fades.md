# 🌌🪶🕊️ Light That Never Fades

_Light does not disappear — it simply moves into silence._

There is a moment when the world grows quiet. When you feel that those you love are near, even if unseen. It’s not an end, but a transition. Light doesn’t fade — it changes form, becoming softer, invisible, yet deeply felt.

We learn to hear silence as the language of memory. The stars above are not just lights — they are traces of kindness left by those who went before us. Each one whispers: life continues as long as love remains.

A feather falling to the ground, light as breath — a sign of presence. We keep these quiet signs like letters from the other side of light.

The light that never fades needs no proof. It lives in us when we forgive, breathe calmly, and give thanks for each day that is.

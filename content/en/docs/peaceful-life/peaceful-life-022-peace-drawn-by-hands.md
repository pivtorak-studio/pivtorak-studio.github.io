# 🧒🏻☀️🖍️ Peace Drawn by Hands

_Peace begins where it is not feared to be touched._

Winter does not cancel asphalt.  
Cold does not cancel chalk.  
And even war does not cancel hands capable of drawing the sun.

Once, children wrote simple words on the ground:  
Mother. Peace. Home.  
They did not know it was a political act. They were simply fixing what felt normal to them.

Today, those same words appear again on the pavement.  
Not as a wish.  
But as a statement: **this is the reality we live in and choose to sustain**.

A child does not fight darkness.  
A child leaves a trace of light.  
And that is enough for peace to remain.

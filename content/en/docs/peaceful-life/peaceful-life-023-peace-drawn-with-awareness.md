# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑📐🤍 Peace Drawn with Awareness

_Peace emerges where responsibility takes the form of care._

Adults no longer draw the sun.  
They draw lines.  
Simple, precise, sometimes strict — but necessary.

For them, peace is not a word or a wish.  
It is a space with a home.  
It is a path one can walk without fear.  
It is a circle where no one is excluded.

They know what destruction looks like.  
That is why their hands draw not chaos, but boundaries.  
Not restrictions, but supports.

When peace is drawn with awareness,  
it stops being fragile.  
It becomes a system that can be sustained.

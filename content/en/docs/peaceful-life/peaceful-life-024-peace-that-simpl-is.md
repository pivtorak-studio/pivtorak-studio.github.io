

# 🐾🕊️🤲 Peace That Simply Is

_Peace is not always created. Sometimes it is simply allowed to exist._

Animals do not know the word “war.”  
They do not calculate threats or build strategies.  
They live — and by living, they keep the world in balance.

When a bird eats from a hand,  
when a cat rests its head on a palm,  
when an animal does not flee —  
it means the space is still safe.

They do not create peace consciously.  
They are **its indicator**.  
If life does not contract in fear, the world is not entirely broken.

Peace that simply is  
reminds us:  
silence never disappears forever.  
It waits until we stop frightening it.

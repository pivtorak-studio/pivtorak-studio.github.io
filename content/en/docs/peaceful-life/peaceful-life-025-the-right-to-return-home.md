

# 🧑‍🧑‍🧒🏠🔑 The Right To Return Home

_Safety as a condition of life, not a privilege or a reward._

Home is not just walls. It is the place you can return to without fear.  
The place you come back to after work, after the road, after a hard day — knowing you are expected there.

The right to return home means that parents do not disappear into prisons, hospitals, or lists of the dead.  
They leave in the morning and return in the evening — alive, whole, present.

It means children grow up with their families, not waiting for another one.  
Their sleep is calm, and their questions about tomorrow are not filled with fear.

**Peace begins where return is guaranteed.**  
**Where life is not broken between leaving home and coming back.**

# 👧🏻🧸🛏️ The Right to Remain a Child | Peaceful Life

_Not about rescue. About nothing being taken away._

Childhood is not a privilege or a reward.  
It is a state where the world does not demand explanations or issue ultimatums.  
The right to remain a child means not rehearsing loss before it happens.

When a child falls asleep in their own bed, next to a familiar toy,  
they are not preparing for separation.  
They are not packing invisible suitcases or adjusting to the idea of “another family.”

The shadow of parental presence is the space where sleep can be deep.  
The night light shines not from fear, but from certainty that darkness will not take what matters most.

**Safety is when the night passes and morning returns everything to its place.**  
**And the child wakes up as a child — not as a survivor.**

# 🐕‍🦺🤍🚪 Those Who Wait Together

_Not about loyalty. About belonging._

Animals do not participate in grand narratives.  
They do not explain, promise, or dramatize presence.  
They simply remain — where life is.

A dog by the door is not guarding.  
It is waiting.  
Not for an event, but for the return of a familiar rhythm — when everyone is home.

An animal walking beside a family is not an accessory.  
It is part of the movement.  
Just like a child holding a hand, or an adult carrying keys.

When an animal sleeps close, touching —  it is not a gesture.  
It is a form of wholeness, where no one is extra.

**Safety is when everyone who belongs to one another stays together.**  
**Human and non-human. Without explanation.**

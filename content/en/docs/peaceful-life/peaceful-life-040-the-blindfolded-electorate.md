# 🕊️👁️‍🗨️🎭 The Blindfolded Electorate | Peaceful Life

![_Peaceful Life. The Blindfolded Electorate. AP | Pivtorak.Studio. 07.10.2026_ © Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk)](/images/peaceful-life-040-the-blindfolded-electorate.webp)

_When reality becomes unbearable, closing our eyes can feel easier than seeing what is happening._

The blindfolded electorate is not necessarily deaf or uninformed. Sometimes the most powerful form of political manipulation is not hiding the message, but making one message so loud that there is no room left to see what stands behind it.

## What We See

On the chalkboard, a dark figure stands before a crowd of blindfolded doves.

The figure speaks through a megaphone: **МИР** — _peace_.  
In the other hand, almost as if it were an ordinary object, is a grenade.

Around the crowd, signs bearing the same word — **МИР** — _peace_ — stand among the scene. Behind them, buildings are already burning, smoke is rising, and the surrounding world is beginning to collapse.

The doves cannot see any of it.

Their eyes are covered, but they are surrounded by sound.

The image turns a political mechanism into something almost absurdly simple: **the message is visible only because everything else has been made invisible.**

## What It Reveals

Propaganda does not always require people to believe that nothing is happening.

Sometimes it is enough to convince them that they should not look.

A loud slogan can occupy the entire field of attention. Repetition can become more familiar than reality. A promise can be repeated until the word itself begins to feel safer than the world around it.

And this is where the blindfold becomes important.

The doves are not shown as creatures without senses. They have ears. They can hear the voice from the megaphone. They can hear the noise of the crowd, the explosions, the chaos around them.

But they cannot see the contradiction.

A word can say _peace_ while a hand prepares destruction.

This is the essence of political hypocrisy: **the public statement and the actual action move in opposite directions.**

The danger becomes greater when people stop asking the simplest question:

_What is actually happening behind the words?_

## What We Choose

A peaceful life does not mean believing every peaceful word.

It means learning to compare words with actions.

When someone says _peace_, we can ask what their actions create. When someone promises safety, we can look at what is happening to the people around us. When a slogan becomes louder and louder, we can step back and examine what it is trying to make us stop seeing.

Peace needs more than a beautiful word.

It needs reality to agree with the word.

> **When eyes are closed to the truth, the word “peace” in an aggressor’s megaphone can become a sentence of death.**

The question is not only **“What are they telling us?”**

It is also:

**“What are we refusing to see?”**

Blindfolded electorate · political hypocrisy · propaganda and destruction · closed eyes · false slogans

**Before:** We hear the slogan and accept the picture it creates for us.  
**After:** We hear the slogan — and look for the reality behind it.  
**Transition:** The blindfold does not have to remain forever. The first step toward clarity is simply choosing to look.

**Important distinction:** Critical thinking is not refusing to believe anything. It is refusing to surrender our ability to compare words with reality.

> Peace is not the loudest promise in the room. It is the reality that remains when we open our eyes.

A peaceful life begins with the courage to see what is there. And sometimes the first act of peace is simply removing the blindfold.

# 🕊️ Dream as Calling
_When you are reminded that you have a Calling._ <br>
**16.07.2023.** A white background. Silence. <br>
And a dove — large, weightless, like an air-filled figure. <br>
In the dream, it didn’t just appear — it touched me. <br>
It was a sign. A touch. A presence of support. <br>
I was reminded that I have a Calling. <br>
Not for the first time. But this time — undeniably. <br>
No doubts, no explanations, no rush. <br>
Just — yes. <br>
Just — here. <br>
After that, I acted fast. <br>
Intuitively — with precision. <br>
Each step was attuned. <br>
And today, when all this has become part of a system, <br>
I remember: <br>
this Calling didn’t come from my mind. <br>
It came from above. <br>
Through the dove. <br>
And through Love. <br>

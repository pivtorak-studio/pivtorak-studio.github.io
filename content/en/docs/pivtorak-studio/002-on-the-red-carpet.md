# 🟥 On the Red Carpet <br>
*My path — is already here.* <br>

**19.07.2023.** This isn’t cinema. Not Cannes. Not someone else’s applause.  
This is my carpet.  
And I stepped onto it myself. <br>

No stage. No script.  
And no obligation to remain in systems that no longer fit me. <br>

The story has already begun. <br>  
I am already walking. <br>

Not on pavement — on a carpet  
that recognizes my step. <br>

Because in this reality,  
the main event — is me. <br>

# 📍 The Presence  
*The formula is already at work.*

**10.08.2023.** I didn’t just walk away from different systems.  
I created my own.

And now every action happens within a new reality.  

I saw the structure.  
And I didn’t name it after myself —  
I called it what it truly is:  
**Pivtorak.Studio**.

It’s not a brand. Not a role.  
*It is the architecture of value explosion.*  
A system activated from within.

When I published my first works —  
everything became real.  
The new reality was no longer just a dream.

Because in this system,  
I am not the event.  
I am the point of presence.

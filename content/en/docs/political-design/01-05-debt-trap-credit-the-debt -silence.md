**Political Design**
## 💣 I. Debt Trap

## 🙊 The Debt Silence

_When truth becomes dangerous, even the walls start speaking louder than politicians._

Debt silence always looks tidy. The living room is clean, the table polished, the politician smiling gently — and as soon as the Business-Children walk in, he quickly presses a fresh strip of wallpaper over a massive crack in the wall. The crack is structural, almost architectural, and from beneath the thin paper glows the inscription: **“$∞ DEBT”**. The politician pretends it’s fine. “See? Much better,” he whispers — reassuring himself more than anyone.

The Business-Children say nothing. They do not need explanations. They know that if the wall is already breathing debt, wallpaper will not save the house. Their distrust is not rebellion — it is survival. They see what adults prefer to hide: the difference between reality and cosmetics.

In this scene, silence becomes the language of generational fracture. Adults fear naming the number, because naming it creates accountability. Children fear the silence, because silence consumes their future.  
Everyone knows the truth. No one wants to be the first to say it.

**Formula:** 
**Silence = Debt↑, Trust↓**
**When truth is hidden → debt expands → trust collapses.**

**Symbols:**  
– **The Crack:** truth emerging despite political concealment.  
– **Wallpaper:** cosmetic fixes instead of structural reforms.  
– **“$∞ DEBT”:** infinite burden, infinite denial.

**Political Design**
# 💣 I. Debt Trap. 🐌 Old vs New Economy

_The past demands tribute from the future._

Legacy economies move slowly, convinced that weight equals value. They treat their own inertia as a noble burden and expect society to praise the effort of dragging outdated systems forward. This belief hides an inconvenient truth: they consume more energy than they produce.

Modern value creation is agile, light, and scalable. It accelerates precisely where the old system strains. Yet the past still positions itself as the “real economy” and insists that innovation must pay for its inefficiency.

The grotesque imbalance appears clearly: the one creating the delay demands payment from the one creating the acceleration.

**Formula:**  
**Progress = Innovation ÷ Historical Weight**

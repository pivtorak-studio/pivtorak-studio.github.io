# 💣 I. Debt Trap. 🦯 Audit Denial

_Problems grow in the dark — especially the ones no one wants to measure._

In many political systems, auditing has become a forbidden act, as if looking directly at the numbers could break the illusion of control. And so, the politician ties a blindfold, taps the floor with a white cane, and pretends that silence equals stability.

But behind him stands the true shape of the economy’s shadow — a massive Debt monster breathing down his neck. It grows regardless of political narratives, swelling even faster when no one tracks its size or appetite.

The grotesque irony lies in the belief that denial is a form of governance. It is not. Facts don’t disappear when ignored; they simply return with compounded consequences. Every postponed audit becomes a quiet multiplier of future risk.

**Formula:**  
**Truth = Audit × (Courage + Transparency)**

**Symbols:**  
– **🦯 blind cane** — deliberate imitation of blindness  
– **Debt monster** — expanding threat hidden in plain sight  
– **blindfold** — refusal to see what must be confronted

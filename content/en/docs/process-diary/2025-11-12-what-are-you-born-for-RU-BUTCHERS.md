# 2025 11 12 ⁉️ WhatAreYouBornFor 🥩🪓 RU-BUTCHERS

This block emerged suddenly — as resistance, as an instinctive protest against the arrogance of evil.  
By late October, the air was heavy with silence. Something was forming — as if the world was waiting for a verdict it didn’t yet know was its own.  
That’s when the idea of a ritual chronology appeared: nine days, nine stages of collapse.

The spark came from a song — “the wolf pack will tear you apart.”  
Not a call for violence, but a reminder of nature’s justice: the power that watches, waits, and acts only when decay has reached its end.

Each day reflected a different state of matter — flesh, smoke, ash, snow.  
The butcher tools lost their form, becoming metaphors of self-consumption.  
The wolves formed the circle — witnesses, not executioners.

While working, deeper truths surfaced: hidden networks, financial and moral corruption intertwined with the aggressor’s systems.  
Now the process is irreversible. Every case already exists in the timeline of revelation.  
All that remains is its scheduled exposure — and the quiet justice that follows.

In the end, only snow remained — and silence that feels like dawn.

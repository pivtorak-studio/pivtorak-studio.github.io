# 2026 01 09 🌐 New Reality. Intellect and Responsibility

It all started with dissecting the ru-empire's power system. But amidst the calculations, an image emerged: "people in expensive watches legalizing evil." 
We realized that evil feeds not only on oil but on the silent complicity of the elites.

It was a prick of conscience. The image of an intellectual who became a decorator of chaos stood before my eyes. A memory from postgraduate school—the voice of a professor sharing thoughts on the meaning of education: "If education doesn't teach critical thinking, it is meaningless." He became the tuning fork that changed the tone of the entire study. We realized: dissecting phantom nodes is impossible without a revision of one's own power.

During the process, I remembered a teacher from school. He encouraged us to overcome "grayness." Once he asked me: "Wasn't one degree enough for you?" Back then, that question made me wonder. Today, I know the answer: **It wasn't enough.** Only at the fourth stage, in postgraduate school, did I hear the most important thing—about responsibility and critical thinking in applying all acquired knowledge.

These degrees do not interfere with each other; they are different tools for solving the same task. Each is a new lens that removes distortion. 

We created the #NewReality triad: 
- about the dignity to say "No," 
- the exit from serving the system, and 
- intellect as the foundation of national security. 
We are not just dismantling someone else's system; we are building our own defense.

Next — the dissection of phantom nodes. We are ready. We have focus.

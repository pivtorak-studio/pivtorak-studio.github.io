# #ProcessDiary — Process as Provenance  
*#ProcessDiary is a series documenting not the result, but the formation of the system itself.*

This is not a diary in the conventional sense.    
It operates as a **tool for recording decisions, shifts, and operational logic**.

Each entry:  
- records the emergence of an idea or problem  
- reflects the process of its interpretation  
- documents the decision taken  
- preserves a trace for future analysis  

**Purpose of the series:**  
- creation of continuous provenance  
- real-time documentation of system evolution  
- preservation of context typically lost  

Within this series, completion is not the priority.    
**Continuity and irreversibility of record are.**  

Errors, doubts, and directional shifts are not removed.    
They are part of the structure and evidence of its reality.  

Each entry functions as:  
- a point of entry into the process  
- a marker of system state  
- an element of long-term value

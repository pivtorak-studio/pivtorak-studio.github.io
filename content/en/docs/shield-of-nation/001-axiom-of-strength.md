## Axiom Of Strength 🪖🛡️⚓️ Military Defense

_Axiom of strength: where the enemy comes with weapons, a warrior rises.  
Unbreakable as armor. Invisible as prayer. Inevitable as dawn._

Military defense is the foundation of the nation’s shield.  
Soldiers, officers, volunteers form the line behind which the people stand.  
Their service is not only in the trenches, but also in the heart of the nation, alive despite shelling and darkness.

Each day of their standing is a day inherited by a child.  
Each day of their struggle is another proof that Ukraine does not surrender.

The might of the military is the might of the state,  
but even more — the might of love for the life they protect.

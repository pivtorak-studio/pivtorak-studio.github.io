## Axiom Of Freedom 🧠🔭📖 Teacher — Photon Of Knowledge

_Axiom of freedom: where the darkness of oppression threatens the people, the light of knowledge arises.  
The teacher is a ray cutting through the gloom.  
A photon opening the path to the future._

The teacher is the first guide into the world of freedom.  
His word and example become a weapon against ignorance and submission.  
Even during war, when schools are destroyed and children hide in shelters, lessons continue.  
And every lesson is an act of resistance.

Knowledge cannot be forbidden.  
It cannot be killed or erased.  
It is like photons of light that always find their way through the darkest walls.

The teacher holds not only a book, but the future itself.  
And this future is the shield of the nation.

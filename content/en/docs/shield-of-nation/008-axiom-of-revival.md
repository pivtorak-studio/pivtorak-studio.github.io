## Axiom of Revival 🌾🚜☀️ Farmer — Guardian Of Bread

_Axiom of Revival: as long as the land bears fruit, the people stand.  
The farmer is the guardian of bread, the keeper of existence.  
The one who sows in times of storm so the nation may have tomorrow._

Farmers go to the fields even when the sky is torn by thunder and explosions.  
They sow seeds on land scarred by war,  
and they harvest crops knowing that every ear of grain is a victory over darkness.

Their machinery moves alongside risk, their hands alongside hope.  
They safeguard the source of life, for without bread there is no strength.

The farmer is a warrior of peace,  
a guardian of sun and soil who grants the nation its tomorrow.

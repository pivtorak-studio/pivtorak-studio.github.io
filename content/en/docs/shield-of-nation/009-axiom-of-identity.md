**Shield Of The Nation**
## Axiom Of Identity 🎨🎙️📜 Artist — Voice Of Truth

_Axiom of identity: where the enemy tries to erase, a voice arises that creates.  
The artist is the mirror of the people and the light of their soul.  
The one who speaks truth in color, word, and song._

Women artists and correspondents now stand on the frontline of truth.  
They capture pain and courage, showing the world who the Ukrainian people are.  
Their paintings, reports, songs, and writings are cultural armor against propaganda.

In their hands — a camera or a brush, a microphone or a pen.  
And each tool is like a sword of truth that cuts through the darkness of lies.

The artist is the voice of the nation,  
preserving memory and shaping the future,  
so that no one can say: “it never happened.”

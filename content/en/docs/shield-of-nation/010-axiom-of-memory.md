_The one who returns the voice of the dead to teach the living._  
_His pen is sharper than any sword, restoring the truth that others tried to erase._

Memory is not an archive, but a weapon. It shapes dignity and prevents the repetition of mistakes. When a historian speaks, he restores the world’s balance — where every truth has its place, and every crime its name.

The Keeper of Time stands between oblivion and eternity. In his hands is an invisible shield made of chronicles, documents, and testimonies that protect the past from being stolen.

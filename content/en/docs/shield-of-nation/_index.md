# ⛨ Shield of Nation

![_Shield of Nation. AP | Pivtorak.Studio. 01.10.2025_ © Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk)](/images/shield-of-nation.webp)

*Architecture of National Protection*

**Shield of Nation** is a research and visual series that explores the state as a living system of national protection.

It approaches the nation not merely as a territory or political structure, but as a multilayered ecosystem of interdependent roles and functions. Each contributes to the conditions necessary for collective survival, resilience, continuity, and development.

The series is built around the concept of axioms: fundamental principles expressed through concrete roles, responsibilities, and forms of action within the national system.

Each axiom is more than an abstraction — it represents a function within the system.

Each role carries responsibility.

Each responsibility becomes an element of the shield.

Through the interplay of analytical text, symbolism, and visual narratives, **Shield of Nation** develops a unified language for understanding national protection as an integrated system — one in which security, life, knowledge, health, solidarity, culture, memory, dignity, and meaning are interconnected dimensions of collective existence.

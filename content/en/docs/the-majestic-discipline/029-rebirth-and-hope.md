# 🐦‍🔥🥚✨ Rebirth And Hope

A glowing phoenix egg rests in warm ash — the first sign of life after the cleansing fire. It is a moment of stillness, when the new form is not yet seen but already present in potential. 

The philosophy of this stage is to value the beginning: in silence and invisible work, the future strength takes shape. Here, a new quality emerges  from natural restoration.  

The inner state is attentiveness and gentle discipline: the ability to protect the new beginning, not rush it, but support it for its inevitable growth.

**The darkest night always comes before dawn.**

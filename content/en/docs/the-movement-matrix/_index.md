# ⊞ The Movement Matrix

The Movement Matrix is an experimental system developed within Pivtorak.Studio to track the evolution of articles, series, translations, metadata, and publication workflows.

What began as a simple spreadsheet gradually revealed patterns, gaps, and opportunities for improvement. As the archive expanded across multiple languages and platforms, it became increasingly important to understand not only what had been created, but also what still required attention.

This Matrix serves as a living overview of the project.

It helps track:

- published articles;
- language versions;
- series development;
- metadata completeness;
- images and descriptions;
- structured data (Schema.org);
- timeline integration;
- publication and indexing status.
    

The Matrix is both a practical tool and an ongoing experiment in designing systems for independent research, creative work, and long-term knowledge preservation.

Its structure will continue to evolve together with the project itself.

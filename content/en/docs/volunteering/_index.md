# Volunteering

![_Volunteering. AP | Pivtorak.Studio. 07.10.2026_ © Anna Pivtorak (Kostyuk)](/images/volunteering.webp)

Volunteering is a way of taking part in the life of a community through practical activities, shared responsibilities and common interests.

This series documents different volunteering experiences in Portugal, exploring the activities themselves, how they are organised, what they involve and what makes them meaningful. From cultural and educational initiatives to social and environmental activities, each experience offers an opportunity to discover the people, places, skills and traditions that shape everyday community life.

Through personal observations and documentary essays, the series looks beyond the idea of volunteering as an act of assistance. It focuses on participation, cooperation and the practical knowledge that emerges when people come together to do something useful.

Each publication tells a different story, revealing another aspect of the diverse ways in which people contribute to the places and communities they share.
